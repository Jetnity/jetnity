## What the design decides

One statically wired private root owns the entire invocation. Independently reviewed global selection precedes exact custody resolution. Historical correctness and accepted-looking Evidence do not establish original issuance; private runtime membership cannot compensate for a missing qualified original issuer either. No external DTO, ID, pin, hash, review key or saved receipt can start at an intermediate stage.

The review constructor starts with `proposal:null`; it shares the exact existing v3 identity core without fake old envelopes, acceptance calls or changed time/preimage semantics. Internal execution retains the actual initial URL, original versus fresh clocks, complete executable releases, phase-A/B context and actual fact/seal before existing outward projections discard them.

Publication is one atomic in-memory bundle: unchanged #855 receipt, separate #861 custody binding and complete exact artifact closure. #861 custody codecs keep their accepted byte forms; #859's generic manifest must not silently rewrap them under old pins. The union conservatively shares #859's existing bounds. A historical reader/storage success never issues live production, acceptance or F8 authority.

The first producer remains legacy/v1 only. #857's schema-2 pure reader and store fail-closed behavior are present on main but do not silently change current extraction, composition, receipt or pin semantics.
