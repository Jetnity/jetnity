## Source-original lifecycle direction — bounded, no indefinite pin

For the **future separate private source-original vault ONLY**, choose a strict **absolute-expiry approach (Option C-like)**, not unlimited reference-based retention. The parameters below are deliberately **engineering ceilings for local synthetic design**, *not* legal/processor approval or permission to store real originals:

- Unreferenced originals: **maximum 30 days** from the immutable admitted origin capture, then eligibility for verified disposal.
- **Absolute source-original maximum: 180 days** from that original capture, including while an accepted Rule refers to the material. Before expiry, source/profile owner must refresh, requalify and separately accept a new Rule/Evidence version or suspend current reliance to `unknown`; an active Rule must **never** quietly extend the old source's retention.
- Maximum interval to recheck a currently relied-on source: **30 days or shorter when a destination's official change risk or law requires it**; use fail-closed freshness caps that can be tightened independently. This is not an assertion that visa law is valid for 30 days.
- When originals disappear or historical decision/withdrawal proof is unavailable, distinguish 'historical metadata exists' from 'historical complete-source integrity can be re-proven'; no re-created hash or deletion tombstone may pretend that deleted bytes survive.
- Backups/PITR/WAL/replicas/exports must be inventoried and their residual-copy expiry, access and restore quarantine independently approved **before hosting**. Live deletion ≠ immediate deletion from backups. Never promise a fixed residual-copy SLA until the connected hosting configuration and provider terms are verified.
- No indefinite retention, no unbounded raw crawler corpus. Hard bounds for items/source/day, bytes/row, total quota and backpressure/stop health must be demonstrated in local tests; new paid storage costs require budget analysis.

The numerical limits are deliberately *maximum design defaults* and may only be **tightened** by Security/Privacy/Legal review without a further product escalation. Lengthening them, retaining a disallowed source, or claiming retention compliance requires a new explicit Product Owner and competent privacy/legal decision. These source-original ceilings **do not select A/B/C or D01–D12 for the six existing global provenance classes**.
