## Adversarial corrections preserved in the final plan

1. **Domain string is not strict host equality.** `www.gov.uk` includes its descendant hosts in current authority resolution. Exact content URL ownership makes the selected representation narrow. Calling it host-only would misstate the implementation.
2. **Department source is not syntactically invalid.** `home-office` plus `www.gov.uk` passes shape; the plan rejects its semantics and shared-host consequences. Likewise `gov.uk` passes shape but is too broad.
3. **A registered profile is not proof of descriptor compatibility.** Generic R1 accepts some well-formed wrong-UUID/wrong-URL descriptors; SQL cannot inspect code-owned profiles or HTTP identity. The fixed verifier rejects them, so payload review must prevent committing them in the first place. No claim that S1 authenticates GOV.UK.
4. **Public and internal failure names differ.** Graph `profile_unavailable` is mapped to public gateway `catalog_failed`; documenting a literal public `profile_unavailable` would be wrong.
5. **No typed content helper exists yet.** A generic transport is not that helper. Future helper ownership and exact output validation are defined; current source helper's required-field validation is not represented as full exact-key parsing.
6. **Request equals final is one binding.** Final expected counts are one reservation and one URL row, not two. The row is ordinal 1 / `is_final=true`.
7. **Rollback is not symmetry.** Source and item calls are independently atomic, not one transaction. Stored representation references also restrict later profile-code removal, including historical descriptors. No automatic source cleanup or profile revert is promised.
8. **Activation proof has limits.** Today's injected profile and missing-RPC simulation do not prove a future deployed head. Mandatory post-merge/default-registry checks remain, as does Production read-only absence verification.
9. **Old PO approval is bounded.** #748's S1 migration approval does not authorize registration. PLAN_READY is not an approval proxy.
10. **Official HTTP evidence is timestamped.** Final URL equality is observed, redirect count is not. Body hashes are research receipts, not content identity pins or proof of legal freshness. No legal text extraction occurred.
